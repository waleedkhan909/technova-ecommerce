Our Origins

Born From a Refusal to Compromise

In 2021, a collective of hardware architects, materials scientists, and industrial designers left legacy tech conglomerates with a bold mission: eradicate planned obsolescence and restore craftsmanship to modern personal electronics.

Today, every TechNova laptop, smartphone, smartwatch, and audio device is engineered from first principles. We design our own custom thermal chambers, test every acoustic cavity using anechoic chambers, and mill our unibody shells from recycled aerospace alloys.
